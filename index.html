What's the job?

What people ask on the phone

How often do landlords need an EICR?

Landlords of privately rented homes in England need an electrical safety report at least every five years, and sooner if the last report says so. We'll send a reminder when yours is due.

Do I need a new fuse board?

Not always. Old rewirable fuses, a cracked casing or no RCD protection are strong signs. We test first and tell you straight whether it needs replacing.

How long does an EV charger install take?

Most home installs take half a day to a day. It takes longer if the consumer unit needs upgrading or the cable run is long.

Do you give a certificate?

Yes. On a real site, this answer shows the firm's scheme details so customers can check them. This firm is made up, so it says NICEIC approved contractor (example).

Do you charge a call-out fee?

No call-out fee for quotes within our area. Fault finding is charged from the price agreed on the phone.
